# Privacy policy

Visionary Assist stores your settings in Chrome sync storage: the sites you enable, which features are on, and your theme and highlight colors. Those settings stay in your Chrome account. The developer does not receive them.

On a site you enable, the extension reads form fields in the page to highlight filled fields and to show dropdown choices in a larger list. Those values are not saved and are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

The extension has no account, no analytics, and no ads. It does not sell or share data.
